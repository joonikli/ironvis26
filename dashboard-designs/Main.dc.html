The reasoning model handled 10 of every 100 requests but cost 65 of every 100 dollars. The small/fast model handled 42 requests for about 1 dollar. A reasoning request cost about $0.25 more and used 4.8 Wh more (range 3.5–6.2) than a frontier request.

The habit that outlasts the model Use a reasoning model for complex, multi-step problems. For everything else, OpenAI advises: “Speed and cost → GPT models are faster and tend to cost less.”
